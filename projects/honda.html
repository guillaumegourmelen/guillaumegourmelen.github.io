Bimanual Avatar Teleoperation

Image coming soon

At Honda Research Institute Japan, I worked within the Frontier Robotics — Avatar Project team — the same team that built ASIMO — as a Permanent Researcher on the Avatar Robot program: a remotely-operated robot intended to let a user perform tasks and handle objects from a distance, with a realistic sense of physical presence.

My day-to-day work involved hands-on testing of the avatar system: teleoperation tests, grasping experiments with the robot's multi-fingered hand, testing embodiment settings, and investigating control issues to improve the overall system. The platform includes a wire-driven hand designed to handle tasks ranging from pinching small objects to firm grasping — the latest version of which Honda is showing publicly at the Humanoids Summit 2026.

I held a permanent researcher position and left the role in December 2025 following legal and financial issues arising from the employer's handling of the work visa process — a failure to meet the required procedural steps on their side that created significant personal and administrative consequences. I cannot disclose technical specifics of the work beyond what Honda has made public.
