JST Moonshot — Cybernetic Avatar

Image coming soon

The JST Moonshot R&D Program — Goal 1 aims to realize a society in which people can freely control multiple avatar robots from any location by 2050. At Keio Media Design (KMD), under the Embodied Media Project led by Prof. Kouta Minamizawa, I contribute to this vision as Project Assistant Professor, leading the Robotics and Vision Augmentation team.

My work focuses on the robotics and perception side of avatar teleoperation: developing systems that let an operator control and feel genuinely present in a remote robotic body. Current platforms include quadruped and humanoid robots. Key research areas include real-time haptic teleoperation, AI-augmented visual feedback (transparent screen systems, robot-eye views), and intuitive interaction paradigms for non-expert operators.

The active Oricafé project combines a transparent screen robot, AI vision, and a physical café environment — exploring how robotic presence and AI perception can enhance human service interactions. Graduate students are being supervised in haptics, teleoperation, embodied AI, and vision-based robotics.

JST Moonshot Goal 1: the program's stated goal is realizing a society in which people can be free from limitations of body, brain, space, and time by 2050, centred on cybernetic avatars that let one operator embody and control multiple robots remotely. The Keio KMD Embodied Media Lab contributes the human embodiment research layer to this national program, connecting robotics, haptics, VR, and neuroscience.
