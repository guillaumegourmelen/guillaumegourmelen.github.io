About

Guillaume Gourmelen

I am a robotics researcher working on physical human–robot interaction — how robots can sense and reproduce human motor skill, not just motion. I am currently Project Assistant Professor at the Embodied Media Project, Keio University Graduate School of Media Design (KMD), under Prof. Kouta Minamizawa, within Japan's JST Moonshot avatar robotics program. Based in Tokyo.

Research

My work sits at the intersection of teleoperation, haptics, and embodiment: building systems that let a robot pick up not just where a human's hand goes, but how it moves — the compliance, timing, and force behind a skilled physical action — and reproduce that on the robot side.

I hold a PhD from the University of Montpellier / LIRMM-CNRS (2022), completed under the JST ERATO Jizai Body project. Before Keio, I was a researcher at Honda Research Institute Japan (Avatar Project) and a Research Associate at Waseda University's Iwata Laboratory.

Teleoperation & physical skill transfer
