Overview

Hybrid Roller–Jamming Gripper

Image coming soon

Developed with Y. Ren and H. Iwata at Waseda University's Iwata Lab, this is a hybrid grasping end-effector that combines two mechanisms in the same membrane roller: an active roller-based intake for fast, off-centre object acquisition, and vacuum-induced granular jamming for post-capture stiffening and shape-conforming hold.

The prototype was tested on eight household objects (pen, marble, gallon jug, plastic cup, paper sheet, T-shirt, pressure gauge, cable) under planar offset and roll/pitch/yaw orientation perturbations, 280 trials in total. It scored 245/280 overall — 70.5/72 on planar offsets, 174.5/208 on orientation perturbations. Ablation trials on three objects showed rolling and jamming address different failure modes: rolling is what lets the gripper recover from imperfect first contact, jamming is what keeps the grip from slipping during lift and disturbance.

Status: Accepted at IEEE Access (Sept 2026) — preprint available on arXiv:2608.20962 ↗. First author: Y. Ren (Waseda University, Iwata Lab).
